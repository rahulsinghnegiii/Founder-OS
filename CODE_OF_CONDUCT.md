# Code of Conduct

FounderOS follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work and what is best for the project's users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@rahulsinghnegiii](https://github.com/rahulsinghnegiii). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
